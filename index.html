What we do

Renewable heating, done properly.

From the heat source to the last skirting board, we deliver complete low-carbon heating systems tailored to your home.

Air Source Heat Pumps

Whisper-quiet, ultra-efficient heat pumps that pull warmth from the outside air — delivering up to 3–4× the energy they use.

  • Full system design & sizing
  • MCS-certified installation
  • Grant & funding support

Underfloor Heating

Gentle, even warmth across every room. The perfect low-temperature partner for a heat pump — no cold spots, no clutter.

  • New build & retrofit
  • Zoned smart controls
  • Works from your feet up

ThermaSkirt Heating

Skirting-board radiators that heat fast at low flow temperatures — the space-saving alternative to underfloor, ideal for retrofits.

  • Above-ground, low disruption
  • Connects to existing pipework
  • Heat-pump optimised (35–45°C)

Servicing & Support

Aftercare that keeps your system running at peak efficiency for years — annual servicing, monitoring and friendly advice.
