What we do

One partner for your whole energy system.

From generating your own power to storing it and heating your home efficiently — we design, install and support complete low-carbon systems.

Solar PV

Generate your own clean electricity with high-efficiency solar panels sized precisely for your roof and usage.

  • Roof & ground-mount systems
  • Smart monitoring & app
  • Design, supply & install

Battery Storage

Store the energy you generate and use it when you need it — cutting bills and keeping the lights on through peaks.

  • Solar & grid charging
  • Backup power ready
  • Scalable capacity

Air Source Heat Pumps

Whisper-quiet, ultra-efficient heat pumps that draw warmth from the outside air — up to 3–4× the energy they use.

  • Full system design & sizing
  • MCS-certified installation
  • £7,500 grant support

Underfloor Heating

Gentle, even warmth in every room — the perfect low-temperature partner for a heat pump, with no cold spots.

  • New build & retrofit
  • Zoned smart controls
  • Works from your feet up

ThermaSkirt Heating

Skirting-board radiators that heat fast at low flow temperatures — the space-saving alternative for retrofits.

  • Above-ground, low disruption
  • Connects to existing pipework
  • Heat-pump optimised (35–45°C)
