# property-001 — two-bed apartment with twin balconies

The first canonical property: a long narrow two-bedroom apartment, hand
modelled in metres from a customer-supplied plan (kept out of the repo —
phone screenshot of a heat-loss design proposal PDF).

Layout (top of plan = north): Bedroom Two (NW) and Master Bedroom (NE) with a
balcony off the north wall between their windows; bathroom, central hallway,
cupboard and ensuite shower room in the middle band with the entrance door on
the west wall; kitchen opening into the large open-plan living/dining room to
the south, with two windows and patio doors onto the south balcony.

Everything that is not the architectural drawing (phone UI, PDF reader
chrome, ThermaSkirt annotation lines, legends, inset product photos) is
ignored by design — this model represents the building only.
